# Privacy Policy

The [High Gain Design privacy policy](https://highgain.design/privacy.html) applies to Paste Amendment. The app-specific details are below.

Paste Amendment does not collect personal data.

The app and Safari extension do not collect, save, or upload clipboard contents, browsing history, website addresses, or webpage contents. They do not read your clipboard. They have no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated servers.

The extension restores Safari’s normal paste behavior on websites you allow it to access. Safari manages clipboard access and insertion; information you paste into a website is handled by that website under its own policies.

Safari requests website access so the extension can prevent webpages from blocking paste. You control that access in Safari’s extension settings and can disable it for any website.

If you contact support or open a GitHub issue, information you choose to send is used to help resolve your request. Public GitHub issues are visible to others; do not include passwords, private clipboard contents, or account details. GitHub and Apple manage their services under their own privacy policies.
