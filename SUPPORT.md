# Paste Amendment support

Paste Amendment lets you paste into Safari webpages that block it.

1. Open the app once and enable Paste Amendment in Safari’s extension settings.
2. Allow website access, then refresh the webpage and paste normally.
3. If a custom editor behaves unexpectedly, turn off the extension for that website.

On iPhone and iPad, use **Open Safari Settings** on iOS/iPadOS 26.2 or later. On iOS/iPadOS 18–26.1, open **Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions**. On older versions, open **Settings → Safari → Extensions**.

On Mac, open **Safari → Settings → Extensions**.

For help, email **dev@highgain.design** or [report an issue](https://github.com/HighGainDesign/paste-amendment/issues). Include your OS/Safari versions, the type of editable field, and whether website access is allowed. Please don’t send passwords, private clipboard contents, or account details.
